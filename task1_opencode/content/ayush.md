## What I think about AI in software engineering today
It's a strong pair programmer for well-defined tasks, but it still struggles with ambiguous requirements — it'll happily build the wrong thing quickly instead of asking the right question first.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Teams will be smaller per feature shipped, but the remaining engineers will need to be better at reviewing, testing, and reasoning about systems they didn't write line-by-line.

## My hot take on AI
A fast wrong answer is worse than a slow right one, and most AI tools are optimized for the former.
