## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI already writes most of my boilerplate SQL and plotting code, which frees up time for the actual analysis — figuring out what the data means and whether the question was even the right one to ask. It's much weaker at knowing when a result doesn't make sense, so human judgment on data quality and context is still doing the heavy lifting.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
I expect a lot of routine data wrangling and dashboarding to be nearly fully automated, with analysts spending more time on framing problems, validating assumptions, and communicating findings than writing code. The skill that matters most will shift from syntax to judgment.

## My hot take on AI
AI can write the query, but it still can't tell you when the dashboard is lying to you.
