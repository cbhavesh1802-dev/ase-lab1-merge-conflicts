## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI is great at scaffolding — boilerplate, tests, first drafts of a function — but it still needs a developer who actually understands the problem to catch when it's confidently wrong. It speeds up the typing, not the thinking.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
Writing code by hand for routine tasks will be rare; more of the job will be specifying requirements clearly and reviewing what the model produces. Debugging and system design skills will matter more than syntax memorization.

## My hot take on AI
AI makes it easier to ship bad code faster, not just good code faster.
